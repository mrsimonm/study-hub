## Overview · Light-dependent reactions | 3 min
- Performed by **autotrophic** organisms; **light energy → chemical energy** stored in bonds of organic compounds made from **H₂O and CO₂** (p. 2)
- Equation: **6 CO₂ + 12 H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ + 6 H₂O** (p. 2)
- Leaf: upper epidermis, **palisade** and **spongy** mesophyll, veins, air spaces, lower epidermis with **stomata** and guard cells (p. 3)
- **Chloroplast**: outer + inner membrane, **stroma**, **thylakoids**, thylakoid space, **grana** (p. 4)
- **Two phases** (p. 5): **light reactions** (thylakoids; use H₂O and light, release O₂, make ATP + NADPH) → **Calvin cycle** (stroma; uses CO₂, ATP, NADPH; makes sugar; returns ADP + Pi and NADP⁺)


### Light-dependent reactions
- **Primary processes**; in **thylakoids**; **need light**; light → chemical energy (**ATP, NADPH**); **O₂ released as a by-product** (p. 6)
- **Dual nature of light**: electromagnetic wave + particle (**photon**) (p. 7)
- **PhAR (photosynthetically active radiation) = 400–750 nm** (p. 8); shorter wavelength = higher energy (p. 9)
- **Pigments** absorb light: **chlorophylls, carotenoids, phycobilins**, which differ in absorbance maxima (chlorophyll a and b absorb strongly in blue and red) (p. 10)
- Light **excites** a pigment electron to a higher energy level (p. 11)
- **4 ways to lose excitation energy** (p. 12–14):
- ↳ Conversion to **kinetic energy**
- ↳ Emission as **light (fluorescence) or heat**
- ↳ **Transfer of energy to another pigment molecule** (resonance)
- ↳ **Loss of the electron = charge separation** → redox reaction (donor oxidised, acceptor reduced)
- **Photosystems** (p. 15–16): pigment–protein complexes
- ↳ **Light-harvesting complex (LHC)** / antenna: other pigments transfer energy to chlorophyll a
- ↳ **Reaction centre**: **chlorophyll a** carries out charge separation and passes an electron to the primary acceptor
- ↳ **Photosystem I (PSI)**: absorption maximum **700 nm (P700)**
- ↳ **Photosystem II (PSII)**: absorption maximum **680 nm (P680)**

## Photosystem I · Photosystem II & water photolysis · Photophosphorylation | 2 min
- P700 is excited → **P700*** → gives an electron → **P700⁺** (p. 19–20)
- Electron path: **P700* → A0 → A1 → Fe–S → ferredoxin → ferredoxin-NADP⁺ reductase → NADP⁺ → NADPH + H⁺** (p. 21–26)
- PSI is now **missing an electron**; it is supplied by **PSII** (p. 26)


### Photosystem II & water photolysis
- P680 → **P680*** → **P680⁺** (p. 29–30)
- Electron path: **P680* → pheophytin → plastoquinone QA → plastoquinone QB → cytochrome b6/f → plastocyanin → P700⁺** (p. 31–35)
- PSII is now missing an electron; it is supplied by **water photolysis** (p. 36)
- **Water photolysis** at the **oxygen-evolving complex (OEC)**: **2 H₂O → 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻ + O₂** (p. 37)


### Photophosphorylation
- **Photophosphorylation** = light-driven **ATP synthesis** (p. 38)
- Electron transport pumps **H⁺ into the thylakoid compartment** (high H⁺); the stroma has low H⁺; ATP synthase uses the gradient: **ADP + P → ATP** in the stroma (p. 39)
- **Non-cyclic flow, the "Z-scheme"** (p. 40): both photosystems; **makes NADPH**; H⁺ transported into the thylakoid; proton gradient → ATP (4 photons at each photosystem: 2 H₂O → O₂; 2 NADP⁺ → 2 NADPH)
- **Cyclic electron flow** (p. 41–44): P700* → A0 → A1 → Fe–S → ferredoxin → **back to cytochrome b6/f → plastocyanin → P700**
- ↳ **Only ATP** is made; **no NADPH**; **only PSI** involved

## Light-independent reactions: Calvin cycle · C3 plants · RuBisCO & photorespiration | 3 min
- In the **stroma**; **don't need light directly**; **fixation of CO₂** and synthesis of saccharides; use **ATP and NADPH** from the light reactions (p. 45)
- **Three phases** (diagram: input 3 CO₂ → output 1 G3P; uses 9 ATP + 6 NADPH) (p. 46):
- ↳ **Carbon fixation** (p. 47): **CO₂ (1C) + ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP, 5C) → unstable 6C → 2 × 3-phosphoglycerate (3C)**. Enzyme: **RuBisCO**
- ↳ **Reduction** (p. 48–50):
- ↳ Step 1: **3-phosphoglycerate + ATP → 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate** (phosphorylation, kinase)
- ↳ Step 2: **1,3-bisphosphoglycerate + NADPH → glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (G3P)** (redox, dehydrogenase)
- ↳ **One G3P leaves the cycle** → glucose and other organic compounds
- ↳ **Regeneration** (p. 51): **5 G3P → 3 RuBP**, using ATP (synthase)


### C3 plants
- The first product of fixation is a **3C compound** (3-phosphoglycerate); usually **temperate** plants
- Stomata regulated by conditions; under **drought and heat**:
- ↳ Open stomata → **water loss by transpiration**
- ↳ Closed stomata → **O₂ rises, CO₂ intake falls** → the Calvin cycle slows and stops


### RuBisCO & photorespiration
- **RuBisCO = ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase/oxygenase**; up to **50 % of leaf protein**; binds **both CO₂ and O₂**; carboxylation : oxygenation = **4 : 1** (p. 53)
- **Low CO₂ + high O₂** → oxygenation dominates → **photorespiration** (p. 53)
- **Photorespiration**: O₂ + RuBP (5C) → **3-phosphoglycerate (3C) + 2-phosphoglycolate (2C)** (p. 54)
- 2-phosphoglycolate can't enter the Calvin cycle; it is recycled through **chloroplast → peroxisome → mitochondrion** (glycolate → glyoxylate → glycine → serine; releases CO₂ and NH₃) (p. 55)
- Photorespiration: **energy-demanding (needs ATP)**, but **protects** the photosynthetic apparatus from **photo-oxidation** and the plant from **free radicals** (p. 56)
- **C4 and CAM** = adaptations to **drought and high temperature** that **avoid photorespiration** (p. 56)

## C4 plants · CAM plants | 2 min
- Hot, dry climates: **sugar cane, maize** (p. 57)
- **Kranz leaf anatomy** (bundle-sheath cells in a ring around veins) (p. 57)
- CO₂ uptake and fixation **separated spatially**: primary uptake in **mesophyll cells**; Calvin cycle in **bundle-sheath cells** (p. 57)
- **C4 cycle** (p. 58):
- ↳ **Mesophyll**: CO₂ fixed by **phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP)** via **PEP carboxylase** → **oxaloacetate (4C)** → **malate (4C)**
- ↳ **Bundle sheath**: malate → **pyruvate (3C) + CO₂**; CO₂ → Calvin cycle
- ↳ Pyruvate returns to the mesophyll; **PEP regenerated using ATP**


### CAM plants
- **CAM = crassulacean acid metabolism**; **succulents** (p. 59)
- CO₂ uptake and fixation **separated temporally**; a modification of the C4 pathway (p. 59)
- **Night** (p. 60): **stomata open**; CO₂ taken up and fixed by **PEP → oxaloacetate → malate**, stored in the **vacuole** (as malic acid); PEP regenerated from **starch**
- **Day** (p. 61): **stomata closed** (prevents water loss); light reactions make ATP + NADPH; malate moves to chloroplasts → releases **CO₂ → Calvin cycle**; pyruvate used to regenerate starch

## Processes step by step | 2 min
### Non-cyclic electron flow (Z-scheme) (p. 26–40)
1. Light excites P680 (PSII) → P680* → pheophytin → QA → QB (plastoquinone)
2. → cytochrome b6/f (pumps H⁺ into the thylakoid) → plastocyanin → P700⁺
3. P680⁺ is refilled from water: 2 H₂O → 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻ + O₂ (OEC)
4. Light excites P700 (PSI) → P700* → A0 → A1 → Fe–S → ferredoxin → FNR → NADP⁺ → NADPH
5. H⁺ gradient (thylakoid lumen) → ATP synthase → ATP in the stroma

### Cyclic electron flow (p. 41–44)
P700* → A0 → A1 → Fe–S → ferredoxin → cytochrome b6/f → plastocyanin → P700; H⁺ pumped → ATP only

### Calvin cycle (p. 46–51)
1. Fixation: 3 CO₂ + 3 RuBP → 6 × 3-phosphoglycerate (RuBisCO)
2. Reduction: 6 ATP + 6 NADPH → 6 G3P
3. Output: 1 G3P leaves → glucose
4. Regeneration: 5 G3P + 3 ATP → 3 RuBP

### C4 cycle (p. 58)
Mesophyll: CO₂ + PEP → oxaloacetate → malate → bundle sheath: malate → CO₂ (→ Calvin) + pyruvate → mesophyll: pyruvate + ATP → PEP

### CAM cycle (p. 60–61)
Night: stomata open; CO₂ + PEP → oxaloacetate → malate → vacuole. Day: stomata closed; malate → CO₂ → Calvin cycle; pyruvate → starch

|---|---|---|
| Location | thylakoid membranes | stroma |
| Light | required | not directly |
| Inputs | H₂O, light, ADP, NADP⁺ | CO₂, ATP, NADPH |
| Outputs | O₂, ATP, NADPH | sugars (G3P), ADP, NADP⁺ |

| | PSI | PSII |
|---|---|---|
| Reaction centre | P700 | P680 |
| Absorption max | 700 nm | 680 nm |
| Electron source | PSII via plastocyanin | water (photolysis) |
| Electrons go to | ferredoxin → NADP⁺ | plastoquinone → b6/f → PSI |

| | Non-cyclic (Z-scheme) | Cyclic |
|---|---|---|
| Photosystems | PSI + PSII | PSI only |
