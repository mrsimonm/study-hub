=== SLIDE 9 ===
Chemical elements
Element
• Defined by a certain number of protons in an atom (atomic number)
Ion
• Has a different number of electrons -> different electrical charge
• Cation: positive charge (less electrons)
• Anion: negative charge (more electrons)
Isotope
• Has a different number of neutrons -> different weight
• Every element has several isotopes

substance.

group of particles that
constitutes this repeating
pattern.

critical (ores of metals, industrial
materials, gemstones)

mineralogical system

Gold
• Formula: Au
• Crystal system: Cubic
(isometric)
• Properties: Metallic luster,
yellow color, very soft (Mohs

quartz veins, placer deposits

currency, investment

Minerals– Silicates
=== SLIDE 70 ===
Silicate tetrahedron
• fundamental building block of
silicates
• central silicon atom surrounded by
four oxygen atoms
• Each tetrahedron carries a –4 charge,
so it must bond with cations (like
Mg²⁺, Fe²⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, Al³⁺) or share
oxygen atoms with other tetrahedra.
