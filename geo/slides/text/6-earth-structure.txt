=== SLIDE 11 ===
Earth structure–chemical layers
1. Crust
• Uppermost layer
• 5 –70 km thick
• Sillicate (basaltic to granitic) composition
2. Mantle
• Middle layer
• Sillicate (peridotitic) composition
3. Core
• Innermost layer
• Fe-Ni alloy
Reflect the chemical composition

Earth layers
Earth’s mantle
• ~30 –2900 km deep
• Mesosphere, asthenosphere,

• Samples only from the
uppermost (lithospheric) part
