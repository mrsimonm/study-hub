Metamorphic agents
Mechanisms of increasing pressure:
1. Burial processes
• New layers of sediment
accumulate.
• Pressure of the overlying layers—
results in increase in confining
lithostatic pressure.
• Metamorphic reactions begin at
depths of ~2 km
=== SLIDE 8 ===
Metamorphic agents
Mechanisms of increasing pressure:
2. Tectonic processes
• Crustal thickening during
orogeny (mountain building)
• Rocks are dragged deeper into
the Earth
• Increase in lithostatic pressure,
directed pressure, and shear
stress

zone” or aureole around the
intrusion.
