## What ecology is | 4 min
**Ecology** is the science of how organisms, humans included, interact with each other and with their physical environment. It asks *why* organisms are distributed and abundant as they are.

- Greek **oîkos** = house, **logía** = study of: the study of the "house" of living organisms.
- Early thinkers: **Aristotle** (384–322 BC) and **Theophrastus** (371–287 BC).
- 18th–19th century: **Linnaeus** (father of modern taxonomy, "economy of nature"), **Humboldt** (founder of biogeography), **Darwin** (evolution by natural selection).

## Darwin vs Wallace | 5 min
:::teach The story the lecture tells
1858: on 18 June Darwin receives Wallace's **Ternate essay**. On 1 July both are read together at the **Linnean Society**. 1859: Darwin publishes *On the Origin of Species*.
:::

| | Darwin | Wallace |
|---|---|---|
| Driving force of selection | competition between individuals | the environment |
| Also | co-discoverer of natural selection | pioneer of biogeography |

They also disagreed about human evolution and the role of sexual selection.

**Natural selection** needs three things: **variation** in traits, **differential reproduction** and **heredity**. The result: the trait becomes more common. Selection comes in three **modes**: directional, stabilising and disruptive. It leads to **adaptation**, **speciation** and sometimes **adaptive radiation** (Galápagos, Madagascar).

## Habitat, niche and temperature | 4 min
- **Habitat**: where an organism lives (climate, substrate, vegetation, resources, other species).
- **Niche**: its multidimensional role and requirements.

:::trap Fundamental vs realized
The **fundamental niche** is set by physiology and resources. The **realized niche** is what the species actually occupies once competitors, predators and other constraints are there. The realized niche is usually smaller.
:::

Abiotic conditions matter too. Temperature splits animals into **ectotherms** (heat from outside) and **endotherms** (make their own heat).
