## Ecosystem services | 4 min
Nature provides:
- **Regulating** services: climate regulation, flood protection, water purification.
- **Material** benefits: crops, fish, timber, medicinal plants.
- **Non-material** benefits: outdoor recreation, aesthetic enjoyment, cultural identity.

## Three kinds of value | 5 min
| Value | Idea | Flower example (Group 9) |
|---|---|---|
| **Intrinsic** | worth in itself, independent of use | it is a living organism |
| **Instrumental** | useful for human needs, a means to an end | nectar for bees → pollination |
| **Relational** | from people's relationships with nature | symbolic meaning, stewardship |

:::trap Easy to mix up
Instrumental = *useful*. Relational = *meaningful to us*. Intrinsic = *matters regardless of us*.
:::

## Costanza 1997 vs Díaz 2018 | 5 min
- **Costanza et al. 1997**: nature's services ≈ **US$33 trillion a year**, about **1.8×** the global economy. Forests ≈ US$4.7 trillion a year; tropical forest ≈ US$2,007 per hectare a year. Total economic value = producer surplus (net rent) + consumer surplus.
- **Díaz et al. 2018**: **nature's contributions to people**, with multiple ways of describing value, not only dollars.

One bee, two perceptions: a **service** (pollen moving, ≈ US$117 billion a year) and a **practice of care** (relationships with obligations in both directions).

Not really opposites: same ground (food, water, regulation), overlapping categories, and both want nature to count in decisions.

**Nature-based solutions**: conserve, restore or sustainably manage ecosystems to address societal challenges while benefiting biodiversity and people.
